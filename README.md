Key Components of a Taxi Booking Platform

Passenger Application

A passenger app can include:

Registration and login

Pickup and destination selection

Fare estimation

Ride booking and cancellation

Driver information

Trip status

Ride history

Ratings and feedback

Driver Application

A driver application may include:

Driver registration

Document submission

Availability management

Ride requests

Trip acceptance and rejection

Navigation

Trip status updates

Earnings and trip history

Admin Panel

The administrative system can provide controls for:

Passenger management

Driver management

Ride monitoring

Fare configuration

Payment records

Service area management

Reports

Support management

Technology Considerations

A taxi booking platform may require integration with mapping services, payment gateways, notification systems, backend APIs, databases, and cloud infrastructure.

The technology stack should be selected according to the business requirements, expected scale, budget, and future expansion plans.

Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to provide businesses with practical information they can use when evaluating taxi app development requirements and selecting a suitable development partner.
